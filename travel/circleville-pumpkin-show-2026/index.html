Circleville Pumpkin Show 2026

01 / ORIENT

Before downtown changes

The Circleville Pumpkin Show fills downtown streets with displays, food, rides, entertainment, and seven parades across four days.

Before the 2026 show setup, Mike and Sherry drove the perimeter on roads that were open at the time, followed the streets used by the published parade route, and continued along the two main show streets. Their drive ends with a surprise stop at Lindsey’s drive-thru for pumpkin donuts.

Firsthand drive, not an official traffic route: the streets shown in the video reflect what Mike and Sherry experienced before the show. Road access, barricades, parking, and traffic control can change during setup and show days. Use the official maps and local directions when you visit.

2026 visitor orientation

These details were checked against Circleville Pumpkin Show’s official website. Recheck shortly before leaving: traffic controls, parking, shuttle service, and schedules may change.

Dates & hours

Wednesday, October 21 through Saturday, October 24, 2026. The official site lists show hours as 10 a.m.–10 p.m. daily.

Parade route

The official route begins on East Franklin at Pickaway, then uses Scioto, Pinckney, Court, Main, and Pickaway streets. Treat the official map as the source of truth.

Open the official parade map ↗

Getting oriented

Circleville is on U.S. Route 23, about 25 miles south of Columbus. Downtown streets become the show area, so review the official show map before choosing where to park or walk.

Open the official show map ↗

Parking & shuttles

The official site currently lists park-and-ride options operated by local organizations, with different days, hours, locations, and fees. Confirm the listing before relying on a shuttle.

Check current shuttle details ↗

Restrooms & help

The official FAQ says more than 70 portable restrooms are provided and an information booth sits beside the pumpkin display. Use the restroom map to locate facilities.
