01 / FIND

Why we went

We were looking for a peaceful walk that would take less than an hour—somewhere with trees, a creek, and a mostly flat grade that older visitors could comfortably enjoy.

Conkles Hollow gave us exactly that: an easier walk with a genuine Hocking Hills sense of adventure.

“The walk was great, and it was cool in the gorge. The scenery—with those tall cliff faces—was outstanding.”

— What stood out to us

WHAT WE FOUND

Cool shade and towering cliffs

The Lower Gorge Trail follows the creek beneath a canopy of trees. Even on a warm day, the gorge felt noticeably cooler, while the tall sandstone cliff faces made this manageable walk feel spectacular.

Hours

Getting there

Use the Lower Gorge Trail from the main parking area for the easier route. Do not confuse it with the strenuous Upper Rim Trail.

Accessibility

The paved Lower Gorge Trail is the gentler option, but outdoor surfaces and conditions can change. Turn around whenever the walk reaches your comfortable limit.

Protect the falls

This is a state nature preserve: stay on designated trails, leave plants undisturbed, and note that pets are not permitted.
