Our Story

Gocation Life began as a quiet escape — a way to slow down, explore Ohio’s hidden gems, and share peaceful travel stories with viewers who crave calm, intentional content.

What started as simple filming trips to waterfalls and historic mills has grown into a full travel brand spanning YouTube, handmade goods, and the GO‑CA‑TION™ van build.

Today, Gocation Life blends scenic drives, roadside Americana, vintage hunts, and DIY projects into a warm, cinematic travel experience.

The GO‑CA‑TION™ Van

The GO‑CA‑TION™ van is more than a vehicle — it’s the mobile studio, workshop, and home base behind the entire project. It allows us to reach remote waterfalls, film historic sites, and bring calm‑travel stories to viewers everywhere.

The van build is ongoing, and every upgrade brings us closer to a fully self‑sufficient travel rig capable of filming anywhere the road leads.

What We Film

  • Waterfalls & Nature: Quiet, scenic escapes.
  • Historic Mills & Covered Bridges: Americana at its finest.
  • Vintage Shops & Antique Malls: Including sourcing trips for Along the Whimsy Way.
  • Roadside Oddities: The weird, the wonderful, the forgotten.
  • DIY & Handmade Projects: Woodworking, crafting, and van build updates.

Our Mission

To create calm, meaningful travel content that inspires viewers to explore, slow down, and appreciate the beauty of everyday places.
