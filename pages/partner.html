Why GO-CA-TION™ Needs Partners

GO-CA-TION™ is a calm-travel storytelling project built around three pillars: Travel • Vintage • Handmade. Every episode explores waterfalls, mills, scenic drives, roadside Americana, vintage hunts, and handmade builds — all filmed with intention and heart.

To keep the journey moving — and to finish the GO-CA-TION™ van build — we’re inviting partners who believe in the mission and want to help power the road ahead.

The GO-CA-TION™ Van

The GO-CA-TION™ van isn’t just a vehicle — it’s the mobile studio, workshop, and home base behind the entire project. It allows us to reach remote waterfalls, film historic mills and covered bridges, explore vintage shops and roadside oddities, and bring calm travel stories to viewers who need a quiet escape.

Your partnership helps us finish the van build, cover fuel and travel costs, upgrade gear, and keep the journey rolling.

What Partners Receive

  • In-Video Recognition: Your name, brand, or logo featured in GO-CA-TION™ episodes.
  • Website + Description Placement: Links on go-ca-tion.com and YouTube descriptions.
  • Brand Shoutouts: Across Gocation Life, Along the Whimsy Way, and JX of All Trades.
  • Behind-the-Scenes Access: Van build updates, travel plans, and gear upgrades.
  • Sponsor Spotlight: Featured on this GO-CA-TION™ Life Partner page.
  • "Powered By" Credits: Top-tier partners receive dedicated segments in select episodes.
