Whimsy Witches • Schattenwald

Leni

When the Lanterns Begin to Glow

There are people who hurry through the evening.

Leni has never understood why.

As daylight slips behind the hills of Schattenwald, she often slows her steps instead. She likes the quiet time between day and night, when the last birds settle into the trees and the first lanterns begin to shine along the old road.

Most villagers know where to find her.

Not because she stays in one place...

Because she always seems to be walking.

Never in a hurry.

Always looking.

The Lantern Path

The Lantern Path begins near the village green and winds gently toward the edge of the forest.

It is not the shortest way anywhere.

That may be why so many people choose it.

Old lanterns stand beside the path, each one cared for by hands that have long since been forgotten. As evening settles over the valley, their warm light quietly welcomes anyone still making the journey home.

Leni has walked this path more times than she could ever count.

She says every season changes it.

The spring smells different from autumn.

The summer evenings stay longer.

Winter carries every sound just a little farther.

She likes all of them.

Dreams Beneath the Moon

People sometimes tease Leni for looking toward the moon more often than she looks at the road.

She smiles when they do.

The moon has never caused her to lose her way.

If anything...

It reminds her that every night tells a slightly different story.

Some evenings she carries a small lantern.

Other evenings she walks only by moonlight.

Neither seems unusual to her.

Books, Owls, and Quiet Places

Leni enjoys places where conversation is optional.

A favorite book.

A comfortable bench.

The soft call of an owl somewhere in the trees.

These things are never lonely to her.

They are simply peaceful.

Visitors often imagine they have discovered a hidden corner of Schattenwald when they meet Leni.

She would tell them the corner was never hidden.

It only required walking a little more slowly.

Leni and the Whimsy Witches

Among the five friends known as the Whimsy Witches, Leni is often the last person visitors meet before darkness settles over the village.

She has a quiet way of making strangers feel as though they have been expected all along.

Perhaps that is why so many people remember their evening walks through Schattenwald long after they return home.
