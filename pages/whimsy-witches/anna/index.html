Anna

Apples • Cider • Cinnamon

Welcome to Anna's Corner of Schattenwald

Anna has always been the one people can count on.

She grew up among the apple trees above Schattenwald, where spring begins with white blossoms across the hillside and autumn arrives with baskets carried back from the orchard.

Now in her early twenties, Anna still spends much of her time there.

She knows which trees bloom first, which apples should be left another few days, and which ones are best eaten before they ever reach the basket.

But most visitors meet Anna somewhere else.

They meet her at the Cider House.

On cool days, its windows fog from the warmth inside. Apples wait in wooden crates near the door, cinnamon hangs in the air, and there is usually something simmering before the first visitor arrives.

No one needs an invitation.

Some people stop for cider.

Some stay for conversation.

Anna seems equally happy with either.

The Orchard

The orchard climbs the hillside just beyond the village.

It has been part of Schattenwald for longer than anyone living there can remember, and generations of families have helped care for its trees.

Anna doesn't claim the orchard as hers.

She would probably laugh if anyone suggested it.

But everyone knows that if you want to understand the orchard, Anna is the person to walk with.

She notices things other people miss.

A branch beginning to bend.

The first blossom opening after winter.

The change in an apple's color that says tomorrow — not today.

To Anna, these aren't secrets.

They're simply what happens when you pay attention.

The Cider House

By harvest season, much of the orchard eventually finds its way down the hill.

Some fruit goes into kitchens.

Some is stored for winter.

And some becomes cider.

The Cider House is one of the places where villagers and visitors naturally cross paths.

There is no need to know anyone before entering.

That is part of Anna's way.

If you've made the journey up the old road and through the clouds, you've already traveled far enough to be welcomed.

Sit down.

Warm your hands.

The cider is ready.

Anna and the Whimsy Witches

Anna is one of the five friends known as the Whimsy Witches of Schattenwald.

There are plenty of stories about how that name began.

Anna rarely bothers correcting any of them.

She would rather pour another cup.
