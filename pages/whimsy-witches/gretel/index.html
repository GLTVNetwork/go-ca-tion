Whimsy Witches • Schattenwald

Gretel

Where Every Story Finds a Home

If you ask the villagers where to find Gretel, they will usually smile before answering.

"Try the Book Cottage."

Sometimes she's there.

Sometimes she's reading beneath the old apple trees.

Sometimes she's helping a child choose a story that somehow seems written just for them.

Wherever Gretel happens to be, books are never very far away.

The Book Cottage

The Book Cottage sits on a quiet lane just beyond the Village Green.

Its cedar walls have weathered gently over the years, and every season leaves a different gift at its doorstep.

Spring brings wildflowers.

Summer fills the windows with warm light.

Autumn scatters colorful leaves across the front steps.

Winter wraps everything in silence.

Visitors often expect a library.

Instead, they discover something much smaller...

...and somehow much larger.

Every shelf has grown a little differently over the years.

Some books are old.

Some are new.

Some have been read so many times their covers have become as familiar as old friends.

No one seems to know exactly how many books the cottage holds.

No one has ever thought to count.

Stories Worth Sharing

Gretel believes every story deserves another reader.

She has never cared whether a book is famous.

Only whether it leaves someone different than when they first opened it.

She enjoys watching visitors wander through the shelves.

People often arrive searching for one thing...

...and leave carrying another.

She says books have always been good at choosing their readers.

The Cottage Cat

Most afternoons, a black cat can be found somewhere inside the cottage.

Sometimes asleep beside the stove.

Sometimes watching quietly from the windowsill.

Sometimes appearing exactly where no one remembers seeing it arrive.

Visitors often ask the cat's name.

Gretel usually smiles.

"The cat knows."

And somehow, that answer seems enough.

Gretel and the Whimsy Witches

Among the five friends known as the Whimsy Witches, Gretel quietly gathers stories.

Not to keep them.

To pass them along.

She believes every visitor leaves with one.

Whether they realize it or not.
