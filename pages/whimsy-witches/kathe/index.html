Whimsy Witches • Schattenwald

Käthe

Where Every Meal Has a Story

If you arrive in Schattenwald around supper time, someone will eventually ask you a simple question.

"Have you been to Käthe's yet?"

No explanation follows.

None is needed.

Sooner or later, every road in the village seems to lead there.

Käthe's Kitchen

The kitchen is never completely quiet.

Bread cools on wooden racks.

A kettle hums softly near the stove.

Something is always simmering, though visitors rarely discover exactly what until the first bowl is placed before them.

Käthe says recipes should never be rushed.

Neither should conversations.

The table has room for one more chair.

It always has.

A Little Mystery in Every Season

Visitors often ask about the old black pot that rests near the hearth.

Sometimes steam curls gently from it.

Sometimes it sits perfectly still.

No one seems certain whether anything is inside.

Käthe only smiles.

"It depends on the day."

Children call it the Witch's Brew.

Adults eventually do the same.

Not because they believe it is magical.

Because every family needs a story worth smiling about.

The name stayed.

Käthe never tried to change it.

Ravens Know When to Visit

A single raven is often seen nearby.

Sometimes perched on the garden gate.

Sometimes resting on the fence after the morning rain.

Sometimes watching quietly from the roof as visitors come and go.

No one remembers when it first appeared.

No one remembers it ever causing trouble.

Käthe has never claimed it belongs to her.

Yet she always seems to know when it is waiting outside.

She usually leaves an extra crust of bread on the stone wall before evening.

The next morning...

The bread is gone.

The Heart of the Village

People often think the Village Green is the center of Schattenwald.

They are mistaken.

The heart of the village is wherever neighbors gather without needing an invitation.

Sometimes that happens beneath the apple trees.

Sometimes in the Book Cottage.

But more often than not...

It happens around Käthe's table.

There, laughter comes easily.

Stories are passed from one generation to the next.

No one watches the clock.

No one leaves hungry.

Käthe and the Whimsy Witches

Among the five friends known as the Whimsy Witches, Käthe reminds everyone that home is not a building.

It is the feeling of being expected.

A warm meal.

A familiar face.

A chair waiting at the table.

Some visitors arrive believing they have discovered Schattenwald.

Käthe quietly knows...

Schattenwald has already welcomed them.
